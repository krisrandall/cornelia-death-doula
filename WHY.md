# Why this site exists

Cornelia is a death doula. She said what the work is, in her own words:

> It is nothing more than relationship building. I'm just creating a
> relationship with a person who's dying and holding space for them. That's it.
> I don't need to over-complicate it.

And why it matters:

> In our culture, across the board, we have fears of death. We have fears
> because we don't know what's coming next. We have fears that we haven't done
> enough in our lives. We have regrets. We have things that we want to tell our
> children that we don't know how to.
>
> When I'm dying, my nearest and dearest will already be in some sort of a grief
> process, dealing with all of their own stuff that comes up. And so I feel that
> I need to be careful not to burden them with more. Which means that the dying
> person ends up not having anyone to process their own griefs, their own fears,
> their own regrets with.
>
> And I want to be that person.

> I'm not here to support the people who already have the support.

Two things had to exist before she could start: **insurance, and a website.**
This repository is the second one. It is not a brochure to be admired - it is
the thing that lets her hand someone a card, and lets that person find out who
she is before they trust her with the hardest weeks of their life.

## What the site has to do

1. Say what she offers, in her voice, without euphemism.
2. Make the free drop-in hours findable, because that is how people will meet
   her first.
3. Give one obvious way to get in touch.
4. Be something **Cornelia can change herself**, by talking to an AI, without
   waiting on anybody. That constraint decided the whole build: the words live
   in plain markdown files, and every fact lives in one small JSON file.

## What it must not do

- Claim clinical or counselling qualifications she does not have.
- State the law about bodies, transport or home vigils as fact - that varies by
  state, and getting it right for the person in front of her is part of her job,
  not the website's.
- Feel like a funeral home's website.
