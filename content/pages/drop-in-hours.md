## How it works

Come and sit down. Tell me what you are facing. That is the whole thing.

You might be dying. You might be caring for someone who is. You might have
buried someone twenty years ago and never said any of it out loud. You might
just be frightened of death and want to be less frightened of it. All of that
belongs here.

I should be honest about one thing: it is an open hour, so there is no guarantee
it will be just you and me. Someone else may be there. If what you need is a
private conversation, [get in touch](/contact) and we will find a time.

## Death café

I am also going to start a death café — a set time, at a café in town, where
anybody who wants to can sit down and talk about death over a coffee. Not
because anyone is dying. Because we have made it unspeakable, and it does not
have to be.

I will put the times here once they are set. If you would come, tell me, and
tell me what time of day would suit you.

## A circle for people who have lost someone

The other thing I would like to run is a simple group for people who have lost
somebody — who did you lose, how did you lose them, and what has it been like
since. Nothing clinical. Just a room where it is allowed to be talked about.

This one is an idea rather than a date. If it is something you would come to,
[say so](/contact) — that is what will make it happen.
