## What happens when you write to me

I write back, usually within a day or two. If it seems useful we have a
conversation — on the phone, or in person, whichever you prefer. That first
conversation is free.

After that we work out together what you actually need, and what it costs, and
you decide. There is no package to sign up to and no minimum anything.

If you would rather meet me before you commit to anything at all, come to one of
the [free drop-in hours](/drop-in-hours) and have a look at me first. That is
what they are there for.

## If it is urgent

If someone is dying now and you need somebody today, say so in the first line
and I will move things around.
